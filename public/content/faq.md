# Frequently Asked Questions – Browse Qatar

## What is Browse Qatar?

Browse Qatar is a platform to discover the best food offers, dining deals, and restaurant promotions across Qatar.

## Is Browse Qatar free to use?

Yes, users can browse and explore offers completely free.

## How do I use an offer?

You can:

- Visit the restaurant directly
- Follow instructions provided with the deal

## Are the offers updated regularly?

Yes, all offers are updated frequently to ensure accuracy and availability.

## What types of deals are available?

- Budget meals
- Buffet offers
- Family dining deals
- Restaurant discounts

## Can restaurants join Browse Qatar?

Yes, restaurants can partner with Browse Qatar to list their offers and reach more customers.

## What makes Browse Qatar different?

- Focus on reducing food wastage
- Budget-friendly dining options
- Centralized deals platform
- Easy discovery experience
